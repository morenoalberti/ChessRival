Study your opponent before you sit down to play

ChessRival is a tool for preparing chess games against a specific opponent. You paste the public link of a tournament, and the app loads the top-rated players, shows you how they play and suggests the line that suits you best against them.

Open ChessRival See the demo

The demo uses fictional players and data, and is only meant to show how the app works.

How it works

  1. Paste a tournament link (or type a player's name). ChessRival understands public pages from Chess-Results, Info64, ChessRoster, ChessRegister and VegaResults.
  2. Choose how many players to load, from 1 to 10, among the highest-rated in the tournament. Each one appears as a card with their profile.
  3. Import their games from PGN files or from your databases (for example, the ChessBase MegaDatabase). Games are only saved when you press "Import".
  4. Study the player and prepare your game: see their openings, their results with each colour, their repertoire changes and the recommended line against them.

What you can study about an opponent

  • Player profile with their FIDE data.
  • Openings: what they play with White and Black, with the full variation name and ECO code.
  • Playing report: results weighted towards recent games, comparison with what Elo would predict, observable patterns and repertoire changes over the last year.
  • Sample quality: detects unreadable or duplicate games and tells you how reliable the conclusions are, so you don't draw them from a sample that is too small.
  • Stockfish analysis of their games to find critical mistakes and lost advantages.
  • Game viewer and opening tree to walk through the line that suits you best.

Frequently asked questions

Which tournament links does it support?

Public pages from Chess-Results, Info64, ChessRoster, ChessRegister and VegaResults. You can also type a player's name.

Where do the opponent's games come from?

From the PGN files you choose and the databases you connect, such as the ChessBase MegaDatabase, which is read without modifying its files. Games are saved in your browser only when you press "Import".

Can I trust what it shows?

Results are descriptive, not predictions. ChessRival shows how many independent games are behind each conclusion and warns you when the sample is small.

Which languages does it support?

Spanish, English, German, French, Italian and Russian. It picks one automatically from your browser's language.
